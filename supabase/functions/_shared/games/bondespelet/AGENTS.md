# ABOUT THE GAME
This is a leasure game for just a few known people, it will not be released wide spread.

## Game rules
A player can choose color anytime.
Roll the die until there is a start order.
Each player starts with 10000 kr.
Preparation steps are done once in the beginning of the game:
- Select a croft
- Plant fields

## Turns
A year is until all players have reached 108 or further.
During one player's turn during the year they can:
- Buy insurance (for the current year)
Steps during one player's turn during the year:
- Roll the die and move that many steps on the track. If a player roll 6 they get another turn.
- Harvest if they are between 55 and 81.

## Year shift
When one year ended and before the next one starts:
- Buy insurance (for the year to start)
- Buy and sell buildings
- Buy and sell forests
- Buy crops and plant fields
The new player order is the one everyone reached the end of the year.

## Win condition
The first player that buy the castle wins the game.
